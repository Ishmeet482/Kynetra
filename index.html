    With your hands

    Open palm
    Move your palm to orbit the camera.
    Index finger
    Point to aim the cursor at a piece or target.
    Fist
    Close over a piece to grab. Move to position; open to release. Move your hand toward the webcam to bring the piece closer.
    Two hands
    Spread apart to zoom in; bring together to zoom out.

    In target practice, aim with your index finger; close your fist to fire repeatedly. Keep your hand in frame with even lighting.

    With a mouse or touch

    Drag a piece
    Move across the camera-facing plane. Scroll while holding to adjust depth.
    Drag empty space
    Orbit the sculpture. Right-drag also orbits.
    Scroll / + / −
    Zoom, or use the view buttons on touch screens.
    Target practice
    Click or tap a target to fire.
    Keyboard
    Focus the playground: arrow keys orbit; + / − zoom; Home resets the view; Escape cancels a grab. Component buttons identify pieces; direct manipulation requires a pointer or camera.

    Camera access requires localhost or HTTPS. No webcam? You can still complete both modes with a mouse.
